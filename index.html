About

Grounded in research, built for production.

I split my time between research labs and shipped systems — building the multi-view data pipelines behind a CVPR 2026 dataset, prototyping multi-agent LLM systems on networked simulation platforms, and maintaining production web infrastructure for a 20,000+ student school.

My work spans generative AI (multi-agent systems, RAG, LLM evaluation & red-teaming), classical ML and computer vision, and the unglamorous-but-critical layer underneath all of it: reproducible pipelines, validation, and logging that other researchers can actually trust.

Outside of research, I'm usually deep in a hackathon build, a LeetCode pattern, or figuring out how to explain a system well enough that I could rebuild it from scratch.
